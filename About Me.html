About Me

My journey in technology began more than two decades ago as a Mainframe Developer. During my corporate career, I worked extensively with COBOL and Excel, using Excel to create detailed data reports that complemented COBOL-generated reports. I later transitioned into Quality Assurance, which further strengthened my attention to detail, analytical thinking, and structured approach to problem-solving.

After nearly a decade in the corporate world, I stepped away from my high-paying job due to health and personal circumstances and embarked on a different professional journey. I trained as a yoga instructor and joined a technology startup, where I conducted online classes and consulted with clients. Alongside these responsibilities, I regularly maintained dashboards, tracked targets, and managed accounts and operational information using Google Sheets. Over time, I discovered that working with data was more than just a practical necessity—it was an area I genuinely wanted to explore.

When the COVID-19 pandemic brought another turning point in my career, I began taking freelancing more seriously. I earned the Microsoft Office Specialist: Excel Associate certification and started conducting online training in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets. I have delivered approximately three corporate training sessions and trained more than 15 individuals through one-to-one online sessions. I have also developed interactive dashboards in Excel and Google Sheets, helping transform raw information into structured reports and meaningful visual insights.

Today, I am expanding my focus towards data analytics across different business domains. I believe that effective analysis begins with understanding the business problem, the domain, and its underlying business rules. A dashboard is not simply a collection of charts and numbers; it should communicate what is happening, why it matters, and where attention may be needed. This understanding guides my approach to designing reports and interactive dashboards.

I also explore AI-assisted coding to support my dashboard development and analytical workflows. For me, AI is a means of improving productivity and exploring new possibilities, while sound logic, validation, and an understanding of the underlying requirements remain essential.

My professional journey has taught me to adapt, keep learning, and approach unfamiliar challenges with curiosity. I bring together my experience in enterprise IT, my practical knowledge of Excel and Google Sheets, my quality-focused mindset, and my growing expertise in data analytics to build solutions that are useful, understandable, and aligned with business needs.
